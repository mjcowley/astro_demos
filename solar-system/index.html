Things to notice: in the top strip, sizes and distances are at the same scale, so most of the journey is empty space and even Jupiter is only a few pixels across. The Earth is about 109 times smaller than the Sun, and about 107 Sun-widths away from it. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach us, and over 4 hours to reach Neptune. The nearest star, Proxima Centauri, would be another 270,000 AU beyond that.
Planets are shown lined up for clarity; in reality they're spread around their orbits. Distances are average orbital distances (semi-major axes) and sizes are equatorial radii. Pluto is included as a dwarf planet. 1 AU (astronomical unit) = 149.6 million km, the average Earth–Sun distance.
