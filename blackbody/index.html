Curves are Planck spectral radiance. B and V are Gaussian approximations to the Johnson bandpasses (4400 Å and 5500 Å), with the zero point set so an A0 star at 9,600 K gives B−V = 0. A pure blackbody is bluer than a real star of the same temperature, because line blanketing suppresses real stellar flux in B; expect the Sun to read about +0.47 here against its measured +0.65. Spectral class is therefore taken from the effective temperature rather than from the blackbody colour.
