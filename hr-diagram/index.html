Tracks are simplified sketches of published stellar models for single, non-rotating stars of solar composition, intended for qualitative teaching; ages and positions are approximate. Radius follows from L = 4πR²σT⁴. The background stars are illustrative, not a real catalogue. The 0.3 M☉ track beyond today is a theoretical prediction, because the Universe isn't yet old enough for any red dwarf to have left the main sequence.
